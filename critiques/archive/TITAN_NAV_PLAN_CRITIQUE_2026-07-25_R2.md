## Open findings

**N1 (MAJOR, new; supersedes finding 8's resolution).** Section 2.1 item 3: *"take the extreme pixel `(ve, ue)` — minimum incidence when `phase_deg < 90`, maximum when `phase_deg >= 90`."* The maximum-incidence branch is wrong-signed. Verified numerically on a sphere at phase 120 deg: the minimum-incidence visible pixel (inc 30 deg) projects in the sunward image direction at **all** phases, while the maximum-incidence pixel is the anti-solar surface point (visible precisely when phase > 90, inc 180 deg), projecting in the **anti**-sunward direction. As written, `theta` flips 180 deg for every `phase_deg >= 90` frame, `a_hat` points away from the sub-solar side (contradicting its own definition in Section 2), and the Section 2.3 arc sector targets the dark limb — exactly the high-phase regime the cohort exercises (`N1516297000_1` "Titan high phase..." etc.). The fix is to always take the minimum. Phase A tests use synthetic theta and will not catch this; it surfaces only at Phase B/E on real frames.

**N2 (MINOR, new; finding 5 partially resolved).** The sigmoid parameters now exist, but the alignment claim is false as stated. Phase B: *"With the Section 5 defaults this crosses the 0.30 TITAN_LIMB reliability threshold at the `min_envelope_diameter_px` decline gate, so no frame emits a feature only to be silently type-gated."* Computed: `sigmoid((40-52)/14) = 0.298 < 0.30`; the zero-occlusion crossing is at D = 40.14 px, and with the permitted `occluded_fraction` up to 0.10 the crossing moves to D = 42.3 px. Frames with D in [40, ~42.3] px emit a feature and are then type-gated — the exact outcome the sentence promises cannot happen (a frame at exactly D = 40 is gated). Harmless to acceptance criterion 3 (ALL_FEATURES_GATED is a sanctioned terminal), but a Phase B implementer writing a boundary test to the quoted sentence will encode a false expectation.

**N3 (MINOR, new; finding 27 mostly resolved).** Section 2.1 item 2: *"`km_per_px` = the mean of the two components of `Backplane.center_resolution('TITAN')`."* The API has no two-component return: `center_resolution(event_key, axis='u')` returns one scalar per axis, and the cited usage pattern (`src/spindoctor/reproj/cartographic_model.py:141`, `float(bp.center_resolution(body_name).vals)`) reads a single axis. The intent (average of the `axis='u'` and `axis='v'` calls) is recoverable, but the plan misdescribes the call shape and its own cited exemplar demonstrates a different convention (u-axis only).

**N4 (MINOR, new).** The reworked Section 4 fitting signatures now carry 7-8 positional parameters (`symmetry_scan`: 7; `radial_profiles`: 8; `limb_radii_from_profiles`: 7), which violates the convention the plan itself binds in Section 1 / CLAUDE.md (*"If the leading logical group is larger than ~5, make all params keyword-only"*). Phase A says *"Implement the Section 4 signatures"* verbatim, so the implementer must either violate the binding rule or silently deviate from the plan — and the Section 7 adversarial reviewer is instructed to flag both. (Revision 1's `symmetry_scan` had 5 params; the growth is a revision artifact.)
