## Findings

**1. MAJOR — The contaminant mask's domain is smaller than the region the fits sample; contaminants in the W-margin ring are unmaskable on exactly the large-offset frames revision 5 targets.**
Quote: "Contaminant mask over the envelope bounding box" (Section 2.1 item 5); ring component: "pixels of the envelope bbox where..."; Section 4: "on the envelope-bbox subgrid". But the grid spans `+-(r_env_px + pad + W)` (Section 2.2.1), the capsule reaches `|t| <= W + r_env + pad`, rays sample to `r_env_px + radial_outer_pad_px + W` (Section 2.3.3), and the limb window reaches `r_env_px + W` (Section 2.3.4). A contaminant whose *predicted* position lies between the envelope bbox edge and `~r_env + pad + 2W` of `p0` (moon sliver beside the limb of a Titan truly displaced by `~W`; ring stripes just outside the envelope; a V<8 star in the margin) is outside the shipped mask array, so the c-shifted read finds it unmasked and no ray is dropped. After the recenter the resampled mask (still an envelope-bbox-sized source centered at `p0`) under-covers the sunward side of the recentered grid by `|d|`. Section 10 reinforces the small domain ("the occlusion backplanes on the same subgrid"). The mask source domain must be the envelope bbox dilated by `pad + 2W` (or the grid extent plus the shift range); nothing in the plan says so, and Phase A test 10 cannot catch it (the test author supplies the mask). Evidence: plan Sections 2.1.5 / 2.2.1 / 2.3.3-2.3.4 / 4 / 10 against each other.

**2. MAJOR — Offset assembly "summed over both passes" double-counts cross-track on recentered frames.**
Quote (Section 2.4): "`(dv, du)` = the accumulated center shift relative to `p0` — `c_sub * c_hat + d * a_hat`, summed over both passes when the Section 2.3 recenter pass ran." Section 2.3.8 recenters the grid at `p0 + d * a_hat` only (cross-track NOT absorbed), then "repeat[s] steps 1-7 ... from the new `c_sub`" — so pass-2 `c_sub` is a fresh *full* cross-track estimate that replaces pass-1's, while `d` contributions genuinely sum. Literal per-component summing gives `(c_sub1 + c_sub2) ~= 2*c_true` — wrong by the full cross-track offset. Correct spec: final-pass `c_sub` plus `d1 + d2`. Phase A test 12 plants a *pure along-track* displacement (`c_true ~= 0`) and cannot detect the double count; test 9 runs without recenter. Two implementers diverge on the exact number handed to the ensemble. Evidence: plan Section 2.4 vs Section 2.3.8; Section 6 Phase A tests 9/12.

**3. MAJOR — `stars_in_extfov` requires a per-catalog call the plan never specifies, and the naive catalog choice is a documented photometry trap at exactly the mask's magnitude range.**
Quote (Section 2.1 item 5): "predicted positions of catalog stars brighter than `star_mask_vmag_limit`, obtained from the module-level `stars_in_extfov`". The actual signature is `stars_in_extfov(obs, config, *, catalog_name, mag_min, mag_max, radec_movement=None)` (`src/spindoctor/nav_model/stars/catalog.py:414`) — one catalog per call (`'ucac4' | 'tycho2' | 'ybsc'`), filtered on raw *catalog* vmag with no photometry correction (bright-end correction lives only in `reduce_catalogs` via `correct_star_photometry`). The mask window (vmag <= 8.0) is precisely the UCAC4 saturation regime — `UCAC4_SATURATION_VMAG_LIMIT: float = 8.0` (`src/spindoctor/nav_model/stars/saturation.py:91`), and `reduce_catalogs`'s own comment warns "a merged UCAC4 star can carry a magnitude several magnitudes too faint" — so a UCAC4-only query misses or mismeasures the very stars the mask exists for; the correct sources are the photometry-reference catalogs (YBSC for V<~6.5, Tycho-2 through V~8), which the plan never says. Sub-points: (a) the rationale quote "exposed as free functions precisely so other models can compose them" overstates the docstring, which says "so `NavModelStars` composes them without subclassing" (`catalog.py:18-19`) — functionally true, motivationally embellished; (b) returned `star.u`/`star.v` are nominal-frame floats (extfov bounds are negative-origin: `self._extfov_vu_min = (-margin_v, -margin_u)`, `src/spindoctor/obs/obs_snapshot.py:86`), so the model must add the extfov margins before painting mask discs — covered only by the generic Section 1 frame warning; (c) the Phase B unit test "contributes a masked disc via `stars_in_extfov`" under `FakeObs` needs real catalog/oops machinery or a monkeypatch, unstated. The vmag 8.0 default itself is sane (first `CATALOG_MAGNITUDE_BINS` edge is 8.0; the star model's own usable range is `min_vmag: 5.0`-`max_vmag: 15.0` in `config_030_stars.yaml`).

**4. MINOR — Pass-2 mask dilation contradicts itself across three places.**
Section 2.3.8: "repeat Section 2.2 exactly once ... (`theta` and `W` unchanged ...)" sends the implementer back through Section 2.2.1's "pre-dilate it along `t` by `+-W`" — but Section 2.1 item 5 says the recenter pass "shrinks the along-track dilation to `recenter_threshold_px`", as does the `symmetry_scan` comment ("`W` in pass 1, `recenter_threshold_px` in the recenter pass"). The Section 7 reviewer instructed to verify Section 2 line by line must fail one of the three; 2.2.1 should be made pass-aware the way 2.2.2 already is.

**5. MINOR — "the recenter pass shifts the mask by the accumulated `(c, d)`" (Section 2.1 item 5) misdescribes the pass-2 symmetry scan.**
In the pass-2 scan the c-shift must ride the *new* candidate `c'` (and does, per the `symmetry_scan` signature, which has no mask-shift parameter — the d-shift arrives implicitly via `center_vu = p0 + d*a_hat`); freezing the shift at pass-1's accumulated `(c_sub, d)` as the sentence says would misalign the mask for every `c' != c_sub`. The sentence is accurate only for the pass-2 *arc* step (`radial_profiles(mask_shift_vu=...)`). The signatures disambiguate, but the normative prose points the wrong way.

**6. MINOR — No fitting-library interface registers the subgrid mask to image coordinates.**
`TitanHazeGeometry` ships `contaminant_mask` "on the envelope-bbox subgrid" plus `contaminant_bbox_ext`, but `symmetry_scan` / `radial_profiles` / `fit_titan_center` accept only `contaminant_mask` with no origin/bbox parameter (Section 4). Whether the technique embeds the subgrid into a full-extfov-shaped array or the signatures gain an origin is left to the implementer; two reasonable implementations differ at the library API boundary the plan claims to fully specify.

**7. MINOR — Pass-1 gate timing vs the recenter pass is ambiguous.**
Section 2.2.6: "on failure the technique returns a spurious result" reads as immediate, but Section 2.3.8 says "Gates are evaluated on the final pass." On a small-disc/large-offset frame (capsule-diluted pass-1 `score`), immediate gating kills the frame before the recenter that Section 2.2.2 was redesigned to enable; deferred gating feeds an ungated `c*` into the pass-1 arc fit. Which behavior `fit_titan_center` implements — and which pass's gate name lands in `gate_failed` — is unspecified.
